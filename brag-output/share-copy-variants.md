## Devpost / YouTube description
Gremlins is a student-controlled advocate agent for campus bureaucracy, built on Campus Cubbies (UCSC). One question goes to every office that matters. Each office's gremlin answers only from its own policy box and cites every claim. The student's gremlin shares the minimum the student allowed, applies precedence in code, keeps real disagreements as disagreements, and escalates to a human on triggers in code. Nine of ten correct verdicts vs two of ten for one big prompt; zero protected fields leaked vs 120; 152 tests. Prototype, synthetic student in the demo. Built at the hack.dev UCSC AI Hackathon, Cisco Outshift / AGNTCY track, Level 3 "When AI Agents Disagree". Campus Cubbies is an A2A service by Adam Smith.
