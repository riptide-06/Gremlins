## 3. FAQ

Q-01 | Can I drop CMPM 120 this quarter and take it later? | You can drop it, but CMPM 120 is usually offered once per year, so the senior track slips by up to three quarters unless we rebuild your plan [F-15] [F-07]. Check your total credits against the campus minimum first [F-13].
Q-02 | When do I have to declare? | No sooner than your second quarter and no later than the campus deadline, which is your sixth quarter for students who started as first-years [F-04] [F-12]. You need every foundation course done with a 2.8 GPA and at most 7 credits of C- or lower [F-01] [F-02] [F-03].
Q-03 | My declaration was denied. What now? | Appeal within 15 days of the notification mailing date with a letter to Baskin Engineering Undergraduate Advising addressed to the Computational Media undergraduate director [F-05].
Q-04 | Can I take a course at a community college over the summer for the major? | Only with Baskin Engineering preapproval obtained before you enroll there [F-10].
Q-05 | Which courses finish the major? | One senior track of two courses: CMPM 170 and 171, 173 and 174, or 181 and 182, and not every track runs every year [F-07].
Q-06 | Can I take 21 units this quarter? | If your UCSC GPA is 2.50 or higher, up to 22 credits is allowed after instruction begins without special approval; otherwise ask your college for approval above 19 [F-14].
Q-07 | Does an internship count for the major? | It can be attached to a field study or independent study with a faculty sponsor; advising confirms the course and, for F-1 students, ISSP must authorize the work before it starts [F-17].
