## 2. Precedence

P-01 | Senate regulations and Registrar procedures override college advising practice. | campus | department
P-02 | For F-1 students, ISSP's RCL decision controls whether a reduced load is permitted. College approval of a reduced load does not protect immigration status. | federal | campus
P-03 | Aid and billing consequences of a load change are decided by the Financial Aid and Scholarship Office. | campus | department
P-04 | Major requirements and course sequencing are decided by the major's advising office; the college advises on load, standing, and general education. | department | department

## 3. FAQ

Q-01 | I am overwhelmed. Can I take fewer than 12 units? | Below 12 credits needs college approval or the Part-Time Program [F-04] [F-06] [F-08]. If you are on an F-1 visa, ISSP must approve a Reduced Course Load first and college approval alone does not protect your status [F-10]. Dropping after the add/drop/swap deadline leaves a W [F-12].
Q-02 | Should I add a fourth technical course? | If you work 10 or more hours a week, Crown advising suggests staying at or below 19 credits with at most two heavy technical courses per quarter, and planning anything heavier with an adviser first [F-13]. Above 19 credits needs college approval unless your GPA is 2.50 or higher [F-05].
Q-03 | What does probation mean for me? | Term or cumulative GPA below 2.0 puts you on probation; a term GPA below 1.5, or probation plus a cumulative GPA below 2.0, makes you subject to disqualification, which the college faculty decide and you may appeal [F-01] [F-02] [F-03].
Q-04 | Can I take next quarter off? | A leave is filed through the college, and an F-1 student must coordinate it with ISSP first because it changes the SEVIS record [F-11].
Q-05 | How do I get the part-time fee reduction? | Apply in MyUCSC through the Undergraduate Student eForms tile with your documentation by the quarter's deadline; the program caps you at 10 credits and halves tuition [F-06] [F-07].
Q-06 | When is the last day to drop without a W? | The add/drop/swap deadline on the Registrar's key dates calendar; after it, a drop becomes a W until the withdraw deadline [F-12].
