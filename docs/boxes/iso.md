## 2. Precedence

P-01 | Federal immigration regulation decides every F-1 status question and overrides any campus or department practice that conflicts with it. | federal | campus
P-02 | Campus policy (Registrar, Senate regulations, catalog) overrides department practice on enrollment mechanics such as forms and deadlines. | campus | department
P-03 | ISSP decides only the immigration consequence of a choice. Academic effect belongs to major and college advising; money belongs to Financial Aid. | department | department

## 3. FAQ

Q-01 | Can I drop to 10 units this quarter? | Not without an RCL approved by ISSP before you drop; below 12 credits without one terminates F-1 status [F-01] [F-02]. An academic RCL is once per degree level and usually first quarter only [F-03]. A medical RCL needs a clinician's letter dated within 30 days [F-04]. Processing takes 7 to 10 business days [F-07].
Q-02 | Do I have to take summer classes? | Not if you are continuing in the fall. Summer is required only if you start or finish your program in summer [F-09].
Q-03 | How many hours can I work on campus? | 20 hours per week total across all on-campus jobs while school is in session, full time during breaks, with no ISSP authorization needed [F-10].
Q-04 | Can I take a paid internship off campus during the quarter? | Only with CPT approved before you start: one academic year in status, declared major, full-time registration, a related course, and at most 20 hours per week during fall, winter, and spring [F-11] [F-12] [F-13]. Apply at least two weeks ahead; ISSP takes 10 business days [F-14].
Q-05 | When should I apply for OPT? | Post-completion OPT can be filed from 90 days before to 60 days after your I-20 end date; finish the OPT workshop first and expect 10 business days for the OPT I-20 [F-16] [F-17].
Q-06 | My last quarter needs only 8 units. Is that allowed? | Yes with a final-term RCL requested by the start of that quarter [F-05], and you still need the university's part-time approval [F-08].
Q-07 | What happens after I graduate? | A 60-day grace period with no work or classes, during which you leave, start OPT, transfer your SEVIS record, or change status [F-19].
Q-08 | Can I take a quarter off? | A leave must be coordinated with ISSP and your college advisor because it changes your SEVIS record; most leaves require departing the US [F-22].
Q-09 | Does a W count toward full time? | No. Credits graded W or NP do not count toward the 12-credit minimum [F-01].
Q-10 | Can I do full-time CPT in winter? | No. Full-time CPT is only available to continuing students in summer; during fall, winter, and spring CPT is limited to 20 hours per week [F-12].
