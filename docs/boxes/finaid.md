## 1. Facts

F-01 | Full payment of every award requires enrollment in at least 6 credits each quarter. Enrollment is verified at the add/drop/swap deadline, not at a separate census date. | campus | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-02 | Federal Pell, Federal TEACH, and Cal Grant require 12 credits for the full award. At 9 to 11 credits the grant is 75 percent, at 6 to 8 credits 50 percent, and at 5 or fewer credits 25 percent of Pell and none of the other aid. | federal | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-03 | Cal Grant recipients must take 15 quarter credits, or 45 quarter credits per academic year, to graduate in 4 years under AB 2248. | state | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-04 | A student enrolled in fewer than 12 credits at the add/drop/swap deadline who is not in the Official Part-Time Program pays full-time tuition and fees and is billed for the part of the aid no longer earned. | campus | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-05 | The Official Part-Time Program (6 to 10 credits, approved through the Registrar) adjusts fees and aid together with no net effect on the student account. Approval after the quarter begins is applied retroactively. | campus | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-06 | Withdrawing before completing 60 percent of the term earns aid only for the days attended under Return of Title IV; after 60 percent all aid is kept. Withdrawing before classes start cancels all aid for the quarter. | federal | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-07 | When aid must be returned, the university returns funds from the fee refund first; if that is not enough, the student is billed for the portion that covered books, supplies, or off-campus living, and campus housing may be billed for part of the federal aid used for housing. | federal | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-08 | Summer aid requires at least 6 credits per session, and summer withdrawals are prorated by days attended from the date the Summer Session Office is notified. | campus | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes
F-09 | Satisfactory Academic Progress must be maintained to remain eligible for aid; the undergraduate SAP policy sets the GPA, pace, and maximum-timeframe standards. | federal | https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/financial-aid-policies/ugrd_policies_conditions.html | yes

## 3. FAQ

Q-01 | What happens to my aid if I drop to 9 units? | At the add/drop/swap deadline, 9 to 11 credits pays 75 percent of Pell, TEACH, and Cal Grant [F-02], and outside the Official Part-Time Program you still pay full-time tuition and are billed for the aid no longer earned [F-04]. Inside the program, fees and aid adjust together with no net effect [F-05].
Q-02 | Do I lose everything below 6 units? | At 5 or fewer credits you keep 25 percent of Pell and none of the other aid [F-02], and awards are not paid in full below 6 credits [F-01].
Q-03 | If I withdraw this quarter, do I owe money? | Before 60 percent of the term, you keep aid only for the days attended and may be billed for the rest [F-06] [F-07]. After 60 percent you keep it all [F-06].
Q-04 | Does the part-time program hurt my aid? | No net effect on your account: fees and aid are adjusted together, and approval after the quarter begins applies retroactively [F-05] [F-11].
Q-05 | I am an international student. What aid applies to me? | Federal, state, and university need-based aid do not apply on an F-1 or J-1 visa; the Dean's Award, the Regents Scholarship, and private loans with a U.S. co-signer can [F-13]. If you hold no aid, a unit change has no aid consequence at all [F-13]. Office practice is that merit scholarships require 12 credits unless the Part-Time Program is approved [F-14].
Q-06 | Is there summer aid? | Yes, with at least 6 credits per session; summer withdrawals are prorated by days attended [F-08].
