## Duration: 25.0 seconds (same scene cuts on the 120 BPM grid: 2.52, 5.52, 8.02, 11.02, 13.52, 16.02, 18.52, 20.52, 22.52)

## Tone
- Preset: polished; creative direction "hype but earned" (user-provided), as in the landscape plan.

## Layout rule for portrait
Headline at the top (68px, two to three lines), caption under it (32px), the real product screenshot fills the middle and lower frame at 980px wide, the honesty chip sits at the bottom. Horizontal pushes travel 1080px, the vertical push travels 1920px. Everything the viewer reads stays inside a 980px column with 50px margins.
